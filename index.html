What we do

The jobs we do most

Emergency repair

Fast help when it matters most

A door stuck open leaves your home exposed. One jammed shut can trap your car inside. We move these calls to the front of the line and get your door working, or at least locked down, as quickly as we can.

  • Priority scheduling for urgent calls
  • Stuck, jammed and off-track doors
  • Your home left secure

Springs & cables

Not a DIY job

Springs carry the full weight of your door under extreme tension, which is why a broken spring is one repair you shouldn't attempt yourself. We replace springs and worn cables with the right tools, then rebalance and test the door.

  • Torsion and extension springs
  • Frayed or snapped cables
  • Door rebalanced and tested

Openers & smart openers

Smooth, quiet and reliable

Grinding, reversing on its own or not responding at all? We'll find the cause and repair it, or help you choose a new opener, including smart models you can control from your phone.

  • Belt, chain and screw drive
  • Repair or full replacement
  • Smart and Wi-Fi openers

New door installation

A new look for your home

Your garage door is one of the first things people notice about your home. We help you pick the right style and material for your budget, then handle everything from removal to final adjustment.

  • Guidance on style and material
  • Old door removed and hauled away
  • Installed, adjusted and tested
